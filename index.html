What Happened

  • U.S. equities finished lower across all major indices, with the S&P 500 closing at 7,457.69.
  • Technology led declines (−5.48%), pressured by higher real yields and AI-momentum pullback.
  • Energy outperformed on crude spike (+14.51%) and geopolitical risk premiums.

Breadth & Participation

Market breadth remained narrow. Energy and defensive sectors (Real Estate, Staples, Financials) captured inflows while growth stocks underperformed. The equal-weight Russell 2000 moved only −0.52%, indicating large-cap weakness dragged the broad index lower.

Key Takeaway: Sector rotation into defensives and commodities signals investors are pricing in slower growth and elevated uncertainty. Watch for breadth improvement as a signal that the selloff has exhausted.
